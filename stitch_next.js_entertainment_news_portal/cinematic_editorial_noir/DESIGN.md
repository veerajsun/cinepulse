## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an immersive, prestige digital publication bridging the gravitas of legacy Hollywood trades with the kinetic pulse of contemporary pop culture fandoms. 

### Core Ethos
- **Cinematic Immersion:** Rich dark-room aesthetics that treat imagery and media as lit projection screens against obsidian velvet.
- **Editorial Authority:** Sharp typographic rhythm, disciplined proportion, and journalistic integrity paired with high-voltage critical enthusiasm.
- **Modern Connoisseurship:** Elevated, sleek presentation that respects diverse narrative verticals—from prestige festival cinema and sprawling streaming sagas to global anime and K-drama phenomena.

### Design Style & Influences
The visual language merges **Prestige Editorial** with **Neo-Noir Glassmorphism**. Layouts leverage dramatic, asymmetric hero displays, deep obsidian backgrounds, razor-thin borders, and vibrant photographic aspect ratios (16:9, 2.39:1 anamorphic wide, and 3:4 key-art posters). The atmosphere evokes a private screening room: ambient, focused, and distraction-free.

## Colors

The palette relies on absolute darkness, structured surface tiers, and visceral cinematic accents.

### Palette Breakdown
- **Surface & Canvas:**
  - `Canvas Base`: `#0A0C10` (Primary application backdrop; deep obsidian noir)
  - `Surface Level 1`: `#12151D` (Card and panel surface background)
  - `Surface Level 2`: `#181C26` (Active surfaces, floating elements, input backdrops)
  - `Surface Level 3`: `#222736` (Elevated modals, hover states, and popovers)
- **Accents:**
  - `Primary (Cinema Ruby)`: `#E50914` (Primary actions, breaking news flags, brand identity)
  - `Secondary (Electric Amber)`: `#FFB800` (Critic ratings, award laurels, spotlight highlights, exclusives)
  - `Tertiary (Atmospheric Blue-Slate)`: `#181C26` (Structured visual grouping and subtle card backing)
- **Text & Editorial Elements:**
  - `Text High-Contrast`: `#FFFFFF` (Headlines, titles, direct quotes)
  - `Text Medium-Contrast`: `#A0A6B8` (Body prose, deck text, bylines)
  - `Text Low-Contrast`: `#636B83` (Timestamps, metadata, captions)
- **Borders & Dividers:**
  - `Border Subtle`: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` (Default structural dividing line)
  - `Border Active`: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)` (Focus states and active card rims)
  - `Ruby Stroke`: `rgba(229, 9, 20, 0.4)` (Breaking news callouts and live coverage accents)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the high-contrast literary drama of `Playfair Display` with the geometric legibility of `Plus Jakarta Sans`.

### Typographic Guidelines
- **Lead Stories & Editorial Headlines:** Always use `Playfair Display` for article titles and high-impact hero modules. Use italic styling sparingly for titles of works, director quotes, or pull statements.
- **Section Kicker & Tags:** Always apply `label-caps` in uppercase format with wide tracking (`0.12em`) above headlines to denote verticals (e.g., `BOX OFFICE ANALYSIS`, `ANIME SPOTLIGHT`, `STREAMING DEBUT`).
- **Body Text:** Keep article text in `Plus Jakarta Sans` at 15px or 18px with generous line heights to preserve editorial ease of reading during long-form analyses.
- **Numeric Data:** Review scores, release dates, and box office figures should be rendered with tabular figures in bold `Plus Jakarta Sans`.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around an adaptable 12-column grid system tuned for multimedia journalism.

### Grid Architecture & Form Factors
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, and `2rem` (32px) margins with a maximum content container width of `1440px`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, `1rem` (16px) gutters, and `1.5rem` (24px) margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `0.75rem` (12px) gutters, and `1rem` (16px) margins.

### Vertical Template Variations
- **Movies & Box Office:** Uses wide anamorphic 2.39:1 and 16:9 hero containers followed by structured 3-column editorial cards with embedded score pills.
- **Anime & Manga:** Employs portrait key-art grids (3:4 ratio) with stacked vertical badge metadata and episode counts.
- **K-Drama & Series:** Incorporates carousel reels, horizontal thumbnail layouts (16:9) with episodic markers, and streaming platform icon indicators.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy, standard drop shadows in favor of ambient lighting, tonal surface stacking, and precise micro-borders.

### Stacking and Surface Layers
1. **Level 0 (Canvas):** Pure `#0A0C10` matte finish.
2. **Level 1 (Card & Module Foundation):** `#12151D` with a `1px` outer stroke of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
3. **Level 2 (Hover & Active Containers):** `#181C26` with a soft top-down ambient glow: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`.
4. **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, and Drawers):** `#181C26` backed by `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a subtle `rgba(229, 9, 20, 0.15)` edge refraction or high-contrast border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.

### Media Overlays
All image containers feature directional dark gradients (`linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(10, 12, 16, 0) 40%, rgba(10, 12, 16, 0.95) 100%)`) allowing text to overlap cinematic stills cleanly without compromising readability.

## Shapes

The interface balances sleek architectural sharpness with modern device ergonomics using `roundedness: 2`.

- **Cards and Major Containers:** `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) creates an intentional, contemporary frame around cinematic artwork and editorial sections.
- **Interactive Badges and Metadata Pills:** Fully rounded / pill-shaped (`9999px`) to separate classification badges from the squared frames of imagery.
- **Input Elements & Small Modals:** Standard `rounded` (0.5rem / 8px) to guarantee clean, utilitarian alignment.
- **Image Media:** Matches outer card radii via `overflow: hidden`, keeping card profiles crisp and unified.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Ruby Red (`#E50914`) background, `#FFFFFF` text, `rounded-md`, 0.75rem vertical and 1.5rem horizontal padding. Subtle hover scale (`1.02`) and color shift to `#FF334B`.
- **Secondary (Ghost Outline):** `1px` border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)`, transparent background, `#FFFFFF` text. On hover: background shifts to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)` with ruby border accent.
- **Critical / Exclusive Action:** Electric Amber (`#FFB800`) fill with `#0A0C10` text, reserved exclusively for festival tickets, awards coverage, or VIP community features.

### Chips & Metadata Tags
- **Category Kickers:** Transparent background, Ruby or Amber text in `label-caps`.
- **Genre & Format Badges:** Small pill tags (`rounded-full`), `#181C26` background, `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, 12px `Plus Jakarta Sans` medium text in `#A0A6B8`.

### Editorial Cards
- **Cinematic Feature Card:** Features a fixed 16:9 or 2.39:1 image frame, `rounded-xl` container, subtle 1px border. The content region displays a category kicker, `Playfair Display` headline, byline with reading time, and an optional critic score pill.
- **Vertical Key-Art Card (Anime & Series):** 3:4 aspect ratio, vertical title overlay, bottom gradient anchor with episode count badge and streaming platform indicator badge.

### Review Score Indicators
- **Prestige Rating Pill:** `#12151D` pill with `1px solid #FFB800`. Features an electric amber score number next to a star or film reel glyph.
- **Rotten / Fresh Metric:** Minimal circular badge displaying dual scores (Critic Consensus vs. Audience Meter) rendered in strict typographic clarity.

### Inputs & Search Bars
- **Cinematic Search Bar:** Pill-shaped, `#12151D` fill with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`. On focus: `1px solid #E50914` accompanied by a faint `0 0 12px rgba(229, 9, 20, 0.25)` ambient ring.
- **Form Controls:** Checkboxes and radios utilize an obsidian fill with a 1px border. Checked state fills with `#E50914` with a crisp white glyph checkmark.
